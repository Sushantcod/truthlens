Classical Ensemble vs. Fine-Tuned DistilBERT

While fine-tuned transformer models (DistilBERT) achieve comparable test accuracy (~99.1%), our soft-voting classical ensemble (Linear SVM + Logistic Regression + Naive Bayes) was selected for production because it delivers sub-millisecond inference latency (0.01 ms vs. 38.4 ms for DistilBERT on CPU) and offers transparent white-box log-odds explainability without requiring dedicated GPU infrastructure.
