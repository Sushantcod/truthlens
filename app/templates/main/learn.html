// THE 4-STEP SIFT VERIFICATION FRAMEWORK

How Professional Fact-Checkers Read the Web

S // STOP

When an article triggers intense outrage, fear, or euphoria, pause before reacting or retweeting. Viral deception relies on emotional hijacking.

I // INVESTIGATE

Open a new tab to research the publisher and author. Are they a recognized institutional newsroom or an anonymous blog registered last week?

F // FIND BETTER

Search if reputable wire services (AP, Reuters, BBC, The Hindu) have reported the same claim. Extraordinary claims must have multiple independent confirmations.

T // TRACE CONTEXT

Trace quotes and video clips back to the original source. Many viral hoaxes use real footage taken years earlier out of its original historical context.

// CONCEPTUAL REFERENCE

Disinformation & AI Glossary

Astroturfing

The deceptive practice of using bot networks or paid accounts to simulate grassroots civilian consensus around an artificial political narrative.

Deepfake & Cheapfake

Deepfakes utilize generative neural networks (GANs/diffusion) to swap faces or synthesize voices; cheapfakes use simple speed adjustments or misleading cropping.

Malinformation

Genuine, factually authentic information leaked or published out of context with malicious intent to cause psychological harm or reputational sabotage.

Echo Chamber & Epistemic Bubble

Algorithmic social feedback loops where contradictory viewpoints are actively discredited or filtered out, cementing cognitive bias and ideological polarization.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Techniques such as LIME and linear log-odds attribution that expose the inner mathematical weights of black-box classifiers to human verification.

Confidence Calibration

Aligning predicted output probabilities with actual empirical accuracy, ensuring the model expresses doubt when linguistic signals are inconclusive.
